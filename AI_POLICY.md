# AI Policy

## Summary

AI tools can help you contribute, but you're responsible for everything you submit. Use AI to do better work, not to skip the work.

## AI is a tool, not a replacement

We know AI is a very useful tool, and we're not against it. But it should be one tool among many in your toolbox. It should not replace you as the programmer. You should understand every line you submit and be able to explain why it's there.

## Don't create extra work for maintainers

Your use of AI should never make things harder for the people reviewing your work. Ideally, it should make things easier. Some good ways to use it:

- Write and improve tests so your changes are well covered
- Do a first review of your own changes before opening a PR
- Catch obvious mistakes, typos, and style issues early

## Make it your own

If we can easily tell that a contribution was made with AI, it usually means not enough effort went into it. Take the time to review, clean up, and improve what the AI gives you. We want your contribution, not something we could have generated ourselves.

## "Good first issue" is for humans

Issues labeled `good first issue` are picked on purpose to help new contributors learn the codebase. Because of this, they must not be completed by automated agents.

If we suspect a PR for one of these issues was made by an agent, we may close it without further comment. In serious or repeated cases, we may ban the account from the project.
